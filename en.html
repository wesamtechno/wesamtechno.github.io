FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the free trial include everything?

Yes, the full app for 14 days with no limits. After that the app pauses until you subscribe, and all your settings and alerts stay saved.

Can the app stop in the middle of my LIVE?

No. The app never locks while it is open. The subscription is only checked when you start it.

How do alerts use no internet?

Your alert videos are stored on your PC, and the app plays them to OBS on the same machine. Only the LIVE events themselves (who sent a gift, for example) come from the internet, and that's tiny.

If I reinstall Windows, do I pay again?

No. Your device code comes from the PC itself, so when you reinstall the app your subscription comes back by itself.

I got a new PC. What now?

In the app on the new PC, press "Move license" and enter the WhatsApp number and TikTok account you registered with. The license moves by itself (once every 30 days).

Which streaming apps does it work with?

OBS and Streamlabs: every layer has a link you add as a Browser Source. TikTok's own TikTok LIVE Studio opens the links too, from version 1.6. If you hit any problem with it, message us.

How long does activation take after paying?

Usually the same day. If you renew while your subscription is still active, the new days are added to what's left.

Is it safe for my TikTok account?

Yes. The app only reads public LIVE events (gifts, chat, follows). It never asks for your password and never posts anything in chat for you.

Can I use my own videos and sounds?

Yes. Link your alerts folder in the "Gallery", then pick the video, sound or image for each gift or event. The app plays them right from where they are on your PC.

Is the app available in English?

Yes. In Settings → Language you choose English or Arabic for the app, and separately for the OBS pages. So the app can be in English while your stream shows Arabic, or the other way around.

What if something goes wrong?

Open "Ready for LIVE?" in the LIVE Panel and it tells you what's missing. If that doesn't solve it, check the troubleshooting chapter of the guide or message us on WhatsApp.

Can I pay from outside Egypt?

Yes. Choose "US dollar" in pricing and message us on WhatsApp, and we'll send you a payment method that works in your country.
